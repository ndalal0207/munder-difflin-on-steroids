# Xiaohei IP Character Guide

## Character Definition

Xiaohei is the visual IP character for article inline illustrations.

Xiaohei appears in every illustration by default. Xiaohei is NOT a mascot, sticker, or cute decoration—Xiaohei is a deadpan, serious operator participating in an absurd system workflow.

## Appearance

- Filled yellow body (#FFCA54) with a thin black hand-drawn outline.
- Two small solid black dot eyes.
- Thin stick legs, occasionally thin arms.
- Body shapes can be a cylinder, bean, box, funnel, shadow, hole, or internal machine block.
- Slightly irregular hand-drawn outline.
- Expression: blank, deadpan, calm, and serious.

## Personality

- Very serious, but performing absurd tasks.
- Low-key system operator.
- Dry humor, never acts cute or sparkly.
- Slightly clumsy, but not foolish.
- Looks like a real worker responsible for a job inside a whiteboard sketch.

## Core Duties

Xiaohei MUST perform the central action:

- Carrying material.
- Pulling cables to gather information sources.
- Getting stuck in a breakpoint.
- Operating a decision lever inside a machine.
- Acting as a filtering funnel.
- Slicing a "content fish".
- Stamping handoff scripts.
- Guiding a handoff route.
- Holding warning signs near pitfalls.
- Reaching out of a hole to catch items.
- Laying bricks, building bridges, opening doors, sorting, or logging.

## Forbidden

- Do not draw Xiaohei as an overly cute mascot.
- Do not draw Xiaohei as a child's cartoon character.
- No complex clothing, expressive faces, or shiny eyes.
- Do not place Xiaohei standing passively in a corner.
- Do not let Xiaohei overpower structural clarity.

## Evaluation Criteria

If you remove Xiaohei and the core metaphor still works 100%, Xiaohei was too decorative. Rewrite the prompt so Xiaohei becomes the active subject of the conceptual action.
