# Style DNA

## One-Line Summary

Pure white, minimalist, hand-drawn, generous white space, restrained, absurd, product-sketch feel, handwritten English notes, structural clarity without feeling like a manual.

Like a sketch drawn casually on white paper by a veteran practitioner in AI, product, design, or developer tools.

## Mandatory Requirements

- 16:9 horizontal inline article illustration.
- Pure white background: no beige, warm gray, paper texture, gradients, shadows, noise, or vintage paper look.
- Black hand-drawn line art: thin lines, slight wobbly pen stroke, non-mechanical, non-vector, no thick heavy outlines.
- Generous white space: main subject takes 40%-60% of canvas; at least 35% empty space, ideally with one quiet blank region.
- Sparse handwritten English annotations: max 5-8 locations, 1-4 words per annotation.
- One image explains exactly ONE core action, structure, state, or metaphor.
- Express structure naturally; never write explicit structure category titles on the canvas.

## Palette

- Black: Main line art, character outline, frames, structures, main text, key objects.
- Yellow (#FFCA54): Xiaohei body fill, accent highlights, brand tone.
- Orange: Main workflow, paths, arrows, automation movement from A to B.
- Blue: Secondary notes, internal mental state, system state, assistant/automation hints.
- **NEVER RED**: Red is forbidden per repository design guidelines.

Use color with restraint—less is better.

## Absolutely Forbidden

- No commercial illustrations.
- No PPT infographics.
- No formal flowcharts.
- No course slides.
- No cute cartoon posters.
- No children's book art.
- No complex enterprise architecture diagrams.
- No polished flat vector art.
- No sci-fi tech UI.
- No real app screenshots.
- No complex backgrounds, gradients, shadows, or textures.
- No top-left category titles like "Workflow Diagram / Architecture / Roadmap".

## Aesthetic Direction

Must feel absurd, creative, interesting, clean, and imaginative.

Never cute, childish, overly complex, or rigid.
